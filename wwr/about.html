Extreme Current Adventures

Extreme Current Adventures delivers safe, thrilling whitewater rafting, respecting nature and guiding unforgettable river memories through expert leadership and conservation.

History

Extreme Current Adventures started in 1998 when a small group of passionate river guides set out with two rafts and a dream to share the excitement of wild rivers. Over the past two decades, we have grown into a premier outdoor adventure company in the country.

Our humble riverside base has evolved from a simple tent setup into a fully equipped adventure hub, featuring modern gear facilities, comfortable gathering lodges, and a dedicated training center for professional river guides.
